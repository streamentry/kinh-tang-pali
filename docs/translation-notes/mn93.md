## Notes

- From-scratch translation (244 segments). The 500 foreign brahmins pick the 16-year-old prodigy Assalāyana ("chớ thua cuộc trước khi ra trận") to defend caste purity; the Buddha dismantles the claim through seven sequential arguments plus the mixed-marriage, mule, brothers-candidates, and the Asita Devala story (seven seers' curse impotent; genealogy back seven generations unknowable; conception = three conditions + a gandabba of unknown caste). Assalāyana goes silent ("rũ vai, cúi đầu"), then takes refuge.
- The repetitive "kiñcāpi bhavaṁ gotamo evamāha" blocks are mirrored segment-for-segment, including the per-segment ellipsis in the claim formula (18.3 carries the root's trailing "…").
- "Ettha … kiṁ balaṁ, ko assāso" = "sức mạnh gì, niềm cậy tựa gì của các Bà-La-Môn" (repeated refrain).
