## Notes

- From-scratch translation (201 segments, including range keys `mn120:4-5.*`, `7-11.*`, `13-16.*`, `21-30.*`, `33-36.*`). "Rebirth by choice": a monk endowed with faith, ethics, learning, generosity and wisdom wishes rebirth in a given company — aristocrats, brahmins, householders; the gods from the four kings through the Paranimmita-vasavatti heavens; Brahmās from one-thousand through a hundred-thousand world-systems (with the gallnut, five-gallnuts, beryl-gem and gold-pendant similes); radiant, beautiful and pure-abode gods (through Akaniṭṭha = Sắc cứu cánh); and the four formless attainments' gods ("trường thọ, an trú lâu dài, đầy an lạc"). In each case: settling, stabilizing and developing that mind; the choices and meditations so cultivated lead to rebirth there — "đây là con đường, đây là hạnh trình". The final case aspires to the ending of defilements and undeluded freedom here-and-now: that monk "không tái sinh nơi nào nữa". Includes the Anupadavagga colophon and its uddāna verse (root-only).
