## Notes

- From-scratch translation (160 segments). Set shortly after the Buddha's parinibbāna, with Ajātasattu fortifying Rājagaha out of suspicion of Pajjota. Ānanda visits the brahmin Moggallāna the Guardian at his worksite: is there any mendicant with all the Buddha's qualities in every respect? No — the Buddha is "người làm phát sinh con đường chưa từng phát sinh… bậc biết đường, bậc thấy đường, bậc thông đạt đường"; disciples live following the path. Minister Vassakāra arrives, re-asks, and probes: was any single mendicant appointed (by the Buddha or by the Saṅgha with senior elders) as refuge? No — "chúng tôi nương tựa Pháp", sustained by the Uposatha/Pātimokkha mechanism ("không phải chư Tôn giả xử trí; chính Pháp xử trí chúng tôi"). Nor is there any one mendicant venerated unilaterally; rather the ten inspiring qualities (virtue, learning, contentment, the four jhānas, psychic power, clairaudience, the sixteen-pair mind-reading catalogue, past-life recollection, divine eye, defilements' ending) are honored wherever found. Vassakāra's Bamboo Grove exchange and his recollection of the Buddha "tán thán mọi thiền" lead to Ānanda's correction: the Buddha dispraised the "hôn say" meditation ruminating with a hindrance inside, and praised the four jhānas. The sutta closes as Gopaka Moggallāna returns to his unanswered first question and hears the reply again.
