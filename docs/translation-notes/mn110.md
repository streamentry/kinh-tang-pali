## Notes

- From-scratch translation (100 segments). On the same Uposatha full-moon setting as MN109, the Buddha surveys the silent Saṅgha and teaches unprompted: an untrue person ("người bất thiện") cannot recognize either an untrue or a true person, while a true person ("người thiện") knows both. Two antithetical catalogues follow — the untrue person: bad qualities (faithless… witless), companionship with likewise ascetics and brahmins, harmful intention and counsel toward self/other/both, the four wrong speeches, the three wrong bodily acts, the annihilationist view negating ten items (giving, offerings, sacrifice, karmic fruit, this/other world, mother/father, spontaneously-born beings, rightly-practiced ascetics), and careless, non-personal, dregs-giving without belief in results — destined for hell or the animal womb; the true person: the exact positives — destined for greatness among gods or humans.
