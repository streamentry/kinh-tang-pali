## Notes

- From-scratch translation (147 segments). When a monk declares final knowledge, the Saṅgha should neither applaud nor reject but question through the "sáu phép kiểm tra" lattice: the four kinds of expression ("điều gì được thấy, được nói lên đúng như được thấy"…), the five grasping aggregates, the six elements, and the six interior-exterior sense fields — each answered by the defilement-ended elder's formula of non-appropriation, ending with the ego question ("chấp ngã, chấp ngã sở và tùy miên của mạn"). The answer is a first-person past autobiography from faithless layman through going-forth, virtue, contentment, sense restraint, clear-comprehension, seclusion, the four jhānas and the three knowledges to liberation-knowledge. The examiners close with the congratulations formula.
