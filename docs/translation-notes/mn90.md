## Notes

- From-scratch translation (172 segments). Four dialogues with King Pasenadi: (1) omniscience — the Buddha rejects the distorted version and restates "chẳng ai biết tất cả, thấy tất cả cùng một lúc"; (2) caste — courtesy precedence in this life, the five factors of striving for the next, difference of effort when factors differ ("con voi thuần hóa"), and no difference in freedom when effort is rightly applied (four fire-woods simile); (3) gods — afflicted gods return, unafflicted don't; General Viḍūḍabha's expulsion question defused by Ānanda (the "son conferring with son" passage) via the domain metaphor and the Thirty-three gods; (4) Brahmā likewise. The king's closing summary lists all four questions answered to his satisfaction.
- Closes the Rāja-vagga with its uddāna (mn90:19.0–19.6, P present / EN empty — translated).
