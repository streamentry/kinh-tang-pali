## Notes

- From-scratch translation (240 segments) — the longest sutta translated so far.
- Four ways that negate the holy life: (1) annihilationism/materialism; (2) amoralism (no wrong in killing/stealing); (3) fatalism (no cause for purification); (4) seven-body eternalism (sword passes through gaps). Each followed by the sensible person's reflection and the same conclusion.
- Four unreliable spiritual lives: (1) omniscience claimant contradicted by events; (2) oral tradition (some well/poorly learned); (3) logic/inquiry (some well/poorly reasoned); (4) evasive answers (amarāvikkhepa).
- Four comforting ways: Tathāgata + five hindrances + four jhānas + three knowledges.
- Ānanda answers Sandaka's questions about whether an arahant can enjoy sensual pleasures (five respects) and whether the knowledge is constant (amputee simile: only when checking).
- Ends with Sandaka sending his assembly to the Buddha.
