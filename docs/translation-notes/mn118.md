## Notes

- From-scratch translation (154 segments), replacing an empty draft placeholder. Setting: the Eastern Park longhouse with ten famous elders; seniors instructing juniors in tens through forties; over the Pavāraṇā-to-Komudī full moons the Buddha praises the "chaff-free" assembly (arahants, non-returners, once-returners, stream-enterers, and practitioners of the frameworks through breathing mindfulness). The fulfilment chain — mindfulness of breathing → four satipaṭṭhānas → seven bojjhaṅgas → knowledge-and-freedom — with the sixteen steps in four tetrads (body: long/short/whole-body/stilling-physical-process; feelings: rapture/bliss/mental-process/stilling-it; mind: experiencing/gladdening/immersing/freeing; principles: impermanence/fading/cessation/letting-go). Each step is mapped to its satipaṭṭhāna ("hơi thở vào-ra là một thân trong các thân" etc.; no breathing-mindfulness for the unmindful; equanimity after wisdom-seeing the giving-up), the seven factors blossom in sequence, and the closing "dựa trên viễn ly, ly tham và đoạn diệt, hướng đến buông bỏ" fulfils knowledge and freedom.
