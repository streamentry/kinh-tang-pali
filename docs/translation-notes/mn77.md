## Notes

- From-scratch translation (390 segments) — the longest sutta in the entire MN.
- The sutta has three major sections: (A) Sakuludāyī's five reasons refuted (8-9); (B) the five true reasons (10-37): higher ethics, excellent knowledge, higher wisdom, teaching the four truths, gradual training practices (4 satipaṭṭhānas → 4 right efforts → 4 iddhipādas → 5 faculties → 5 powers → 7 bojjhaṅgas → 8fold path → 8 liberations → 8 masteries → 10 kasinas → 4 jhānas with similes → insight knowledges → mind-made body → psychic powers → clairaudience → mind-reading → past lives → divine eye → ending of defilements).
- The Pūraṇa Kassapa and Nigaṇṭha Nāṭaputta episodes (6.18-6.54) show their disciples not respecting them, contrasted with Gotama's disciples.
