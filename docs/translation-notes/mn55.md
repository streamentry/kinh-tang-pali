## High-risk audit points

- The threefold meat rule stays exactly scoped to seen/heard/suspected killing specifically for the recipient, and the inverse three conditions.
- The invited-meal passage preserves non-attachment to fine food and the absence of intention to harm self, others, or both.
- Loving-kindness, compassion, rejoicing, and equanimity remain the four brahmavihāra contexts rather than being collapsed into generic kindness.
- `nissaraṇa` is rendered conservatively as “sự thoát ly”, without inserting an object that the Pāli does not specify.
- The five demerit grounds for deliberately slaughtering an animal for a Tathāgata or disciple remain five separate causal steps.
- `kappiya/akappiya` is treated as allowance under monastic discipline, not as a modern legal or nutritional judgment.
