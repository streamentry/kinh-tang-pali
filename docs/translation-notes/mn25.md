## Notes

- From-scratch retranslation (199 segments), replacing a damaged draft (81 audit issues: fabricated ellipses at 4.10/5.4/5.10, 48 quote-count mismatches). The sower does not bait deer for their benefit but so they become "me say xông vào — loạn trí — phóng dật — tùy tiện bị sai khiến". Four herds of deer map onto four groups of ascetics and brahmins: (1) those indulging in Māra's bait of worldly pleasures; (2) those abstaining totally and going deep into the wilderness (with the herb-eating list), losing strength and — in the human version — "tâm giải thoát", then returning to the bait; (3) those living near the bait eating carefully — exposed by the high-net surround (human version: still caught in the eternity/infinity/soul/world views); (4) those living where Māra and his assembly cannot go — the nine attainments from first jhāna through cessation, each closing with "đã làm Māra mù mắt, đã triệt hạ mất dấu vết, đã làm mất mắt Māra, đã đi đến chỗ không bị ác ma thấy" and finally "đã vượt qua sự bám víu trong thế gian".
