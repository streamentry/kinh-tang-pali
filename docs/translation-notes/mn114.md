## Notes

- From-scratch translation (373 segments, including range keys `mn114:17-19.*`, `mn114:32-36.*`, `mn114:51-58.*`). Three cycles of brief-statement → Sāriputta's detailed elaboration → the Buddha's "Tốt lắm" and restatement: (1) bodily, verbal and mental conduct, arising of thought, acquisitions of perception, view, and individuality; (2) the six sense objects; (3) robes, almsfood, lodging, village, town, city, province, and persons. The single criterion throughout: what makes "các bất thiện pháp tăng trưởng, các thiện pháp suy giảm" is not to be cultivated; what makes the reverse is. Concrete contents: the three bodily wrongdoings (with the full protected-women list), the four wrong speeches (with the witness-stand definition of lying), ill will's "mong các chúng sinh này bị giết…", the ten-item annihilationist/right views (mn110 wording), and the hurtful/not-hurtful incarnation distinction. Closes with the universalization formula over the four castes and the whole world with its gods, Māras and Brahmās.
