## Notes

- From-scratch translation (104 segments). The accountant brahmin asks whether this teaching has "sự học tập thứ lớp, sự thực hành thứ lớp, sự hành đạo thứ lớp" like staircases, brahminic recitation, archery and counting ("một một… mười mười", up to a hundred and beyond). The Buddha answers with the horse-trainer simile and the eight imperatival steps ("Hãy đến đây, này tỳ-kheo…"): virtue/Pātimokkha, sense-door guarding, moderate eating (with the whole reflective formula), wakefulness, mindfulness-and-clear-comprehension, and secluded lodging, each introduced by "Khi nào… rồi Như Lai điều phục vị ấy xa hơn" — followed by the absorptions (9–10). He teaches trainees thus; for the perfected ones these same things conduce to blissful abiding here-and-now. Asked why some disciples reach final Nibbāna and others don't, the Buddha draws out the Rājagaha road directions (one goes astray facing backwards, one arrives safely) and concludes "Như Lai chỉ là người chỉ đường". The brahmin contrasts faithless livelihood-motivated ordainers with faithful family sons (two antithetical attribute catalogues), praises the advice as foremost among contemporary teachings via the root/heartwood/flower fragrance triad, and goes for refuge.
