## High-risk audit points

- The trainee's path is kept structurally complete: ethics, sense restraint, moderation in eating, wakefulness, seven good qualities, and ready access to four jhānas.
- The seven qualities remain distinct: faith, moral shame (`hiri`), fear of wrongdoing (`ottappa`), learning, energy, mindfulness, and penetrative wisdom.
- `apuccaṇḍatā` and the hen/chick simile are preserved: mature conditions make breaking out possible; the text does not reduce awakening to wishing.
- The three `abhinibbhidā` are recollection of past lives, knowledge of beings' death/rebirth according to kamma, and direct realization of undefiled mind-release and wisdom-release.
- The final distinction between `caraṇa` (conduct) and `vijjā` (knowledge) is preserved rather than flattened into a single virtue list.
- Supernormal and rebirth claims are translated as textual claims, not silently allegorized or naturalized.
