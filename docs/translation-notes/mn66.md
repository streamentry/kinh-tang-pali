## Notes

- From-scratch translation (202 segments).
- Udāyī's gratitude narrative (night-meal renunciation, the lightning/goblin anecdote) kept verbatim including the odd parental curse.
- Core structural pairs: quail bound by rotten vine vs royal bull elephant with strong harness; poor man vs rich householder — the same bond is weak or strong depending on the person's capacity.
- Four individuals graded by `upadhi` (attachments) with `indriyavemattatā` (disparity of faculties) as the Buddha's stated basis.
- The ladder of `iñjita/aniñjita` (perturbable/imperturbable) and the repeated "chưa đủ — hãy từ bỏ — hãy vượt qua" ladder up to cessation, with even the neither-perception-nor-non-perception recommended for giving up (34.2).
