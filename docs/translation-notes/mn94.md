## Notes

- From-scratch translation (203 segments). The brahmin Ghoṭamukha opens claiming "chẳng có đời du sĩ như pháp"; Udena's four individuals (self-mortifier, other-mortifier, both, neither) lead him to endorse the fourth, admit his opening line "có cái cớ của nó", and request the detailed exposition. The fourth individual is the full gradual-training scheme: Tathāgata arises → going forth → spectrum of ethics (+ the extra-list of 16.7–16.25) → contentment like a bird → sense restraint → situational awareness → seclusion → five hindrances → four jhānas → three knowledges → "sống với thân là Phạm thiên" (brahmabhūtena attanā viharati).
- Ends with refuge redirected to the quenched Buddha (as in MN 84), the refused 500-kahāpaṇa stipend, and the founding of the Ghoṭamukhī assembly hall at Pāṭaliputta.
- The ascetic-practices catalogue (10.2–10.14) follows TMC's canonical Vietnamese for the naked-ascetic formula.
