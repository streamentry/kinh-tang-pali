## Notes

- From-scratch translation (205 segments). Keṇiya the matted-hair ascetic invites the Buddha (three-fold request, "grand sacrifice" as metaphor for the meal); Sela the brahmin teacher of 300 students investigates, verifies the marks (psychic display of the two doubted marks), extols the Buddha in fitting verses, hears "Ta là vị vua, vua Chánh pháp vô thượng", asks who keeps the wheel rolling (Sāriputta), converts with his 300 students, and — arahant after seven days — offers the closing verses ("duỗi đôi chân… để các bậc đại tượng đảnh lễ Đạo sư").
- The thirty-two marks / two-destinies passages reuse the canonical wording established at MN 91; the "abhiññeyyaṁ abhiññātaṁ" verse likewise.
- Buddha's appreciation verses to Keṇiya (26.1–26.8): the six "foremost" comparisons culminating in the Sangha.
