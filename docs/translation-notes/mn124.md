## Notes

- From-scratch translation (71 segments, zero exact corpus matches; range keys `mn124:4-5.*`, `7-8.*`, `11-15.*`, `16-19.*`, `20-25.*`, `26-29.*`, `30-36.*`). Structure: the naked ascetic Kassapa, Bakkula's old lay-life friend, asks how long he has been ordained (eighty years) and how many times he has had sex; Bakkula rejects the question's framing and redirects it to sensual perceptions. The body of the sutta is a series of "in these eighty years I don't recall ever …" claims — sensual/ill-will/cruelty perceptions and thoughts, accepting householder robes, cutting/sewing/dyeing robes and robe-making (kathina), invitations and the wish "‘Mong rằng có ai đó thỉnh mời ta!’", sitting or eating in a house, taking in the details of a woman's appearance or teaching women (even a four-line verse), visiting or teaching nuns/trainees/novices, giving going-forth/ordination/dependence, being attended by a novice, sauna- and powder-bathing, massages from companions, illness (even a cow's-milking instant), medicine (even a morsel of harītakī), leaning-boards and making beds, and rains-retreat near a village — each sealed by the interlocutor's recap "việc này chúng tôi thọ trì là một pháp hy hữu, một pháp vị tằng hữu của Tôn giả Bakkula". Then the seven-days-indebted alms-eating with final knowledge arising on the eighth day; Kassapa's ordination and swift arahantship; and Bakkula's key-in-hand farewell tour ("Chư Tôn giả hãy đi ra! … Hôm nay tôi sẽ nhập Niết-bàn") ending in his seated parinibbāna amid the Saṅgha.
