## Notes

- From-scratch translation (107 segments, zero exact corpus matches — a unique sutta). Structure: monks in the assembly hall marvel that the Tathāgata can know the fully quenched Buddhas of the past (cut off proliferation, cut off the course, finished the cycle, passed beyond suffering) in eight respects — birth, names, clans, conduct, Dhamma, wisdom, dwelling, freedom; Ānanda adds that Tathāgatas are "incredible with incredible qualities, amazing with amazing qualities". The Buddha, arriving, asks Ānanda to say more, and Ānanda recites from direct hearsay ("con đã nghe ngay trước mặt Đức Phật, đã thọ trì ngay trước mặt Đức Phật:") twenty wonders of the bodhisattva: mindful-and-aware rebirth, abiding and life-long abiding in the Tusita host; mindful descent into the womb with the immeasurable light shaking the ten-thousandfold world (repeated verbatim at birth); four deva-guardians of the quarters; the mother's natural virtue (five precepts), freedom from sexual thoughts and violation, enjoyment of the five sense-pleasures, health and the womb-vision (eight-faceted beryl-gem-with-colored-thread simile); her passing seven days after birth to Tusita; exactly ten months' gestation; standing delivery; gods receiving him first; the four devas presenting him ("hãy hoan hỷ, hoàng hậu!..."); emergence clean (jewel-on-Kāsi-cloth simile); the cool and warm water showers; the seven strides with white parasol and the bull-voiced proclamation "Ta là bậc tối thượng của thế giới..."; and the light again. The Buddha then contributes the twenty-first wonder — the Tathāgata's clear knowledge of feelings, perceptions and thoughts as they arise, stand and pass away — which Ānanda repeats back; the Teacher concurs.
