## Notes

- From-scratch translation (198 segments). Frame: the Buddha smiles on the road (2), tells Ānanda of the past Buddha Kassapa's monastery at that spot (3–5), then the long flashback: the potter Ghaṭīkāra drags his reluctant brahmin-student friend Jotipāla (three refusals, bathing trip, skirt-hem and hair-grabbing) to see Buddha Kassapa (6–10); Jotipāla ordains (11–12); King Kikī's failed rains invitation (13–17); the king asks who the Buddha's chief supporter is and hears Ghaṭīkāra's virtues: refuge, five precepts, fourfold experiential confidence, no doubt in the four truths, one meal a day, no gold, no digging earth, barter-style pot exchange, and care for blind old parents — a once-returner? no: a non-returner who will be spontaneously reborn (18); three anecdotes of the Buddha's trust (rice from the pot, porridge, the grass roof of the workshop stripped with a rainless sky for three months) (19–21); Jotipāla was the Buddha himself (23.4).
- "yāvatādohipi" is a known crux: Sujato reads the milk-idiom ("you'd even milk it to this extent"), TMC reads "có thật sự là cần thiết không?" — the translation adopts a neutral middle ("cần phải làm đến mức ấy sao") and the divergence is documented in the comment file.
- "bhaggavo" rendered per TMC as the generic "người thợ gốm ấy"; Sujato treats it as a nickname (Bhaggava) — documented.
- Repetition frames "Dutiyampi …pe…" (6.9, 7.8, 17.9) kept as skeletons with ellipsis per root.
- Vocatives: "samma" = "bạn" between the friends; "tāta" = "này con" (parents to son); "bhagini" = "này chị"; king ↔ Buddha: "Đại vương" / "bạch Thế Tôn".
