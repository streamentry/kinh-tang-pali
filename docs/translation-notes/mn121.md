## Notes

- From-scratch translation (102 segments). Structure: Ānanda asks the Buddha to confirm the saying heard at Nagaraka ("nay Ta thường an trú bằng pháp không"); the Buddha confirms, then unfolds the progressive series of emptiness — the Migāra's-mother palace simile (empty of elephants/cattle/horses/mares, gold/silver, gatherings of men and women; the not-empty remainder is the oneness dependent on the mendicant Saṅgha), then nine levels: wilderness → earth (bull-hide-on-a-hundred-pegs simile) → infinite space → infinite consciousness → nothingness → neither-perception-nor-non-perception → signless immersion twice → emptiness applied to liberation itself. Each level: drop the two lower perceptions, attend to the oneness dependent on the higher one ("tâm hướng đến, tịnh tín, an trú, quyết định"); the stresses due to the dropped perceptions are absent, a modicum remains ("một chút não hại này"); the field of perception is empty of them ("lĩnh vực tưởng này trống không tưởng X"); what is absent is seen as empty, what remains is known as present ("‘Cái này có mặt’"); the manifestation of emptiness is genuine, undistorted, pure (level 12 adds "tối thượng vô thượng"). Level 11 pivots on "even the signless immersion is produced by choices and intentions … impermanent, liable to cessation", freeing the mind from the three cankers; level 12 leaves only the six-sense-field body conditioned by life. Conclusion: all ascetics and brahmins past, future and present who enter the pure, ultimate, supreme emptiness enter this very one; closing exhortation.
