## Notes

- From-scratch translation (171 segments). King Avantiputta asks Mahākaccāna about the brahmins' birth-privilege claim; five ways to see it is "chỉ là lời đồn ở đời" (ghosoyeva, TMC "một âm thanh ở đời"): prosperity makes servants of all castes; evil deeds send all castes to hell; good deeds raise all to heaven; crime strips caste and leaves only "kẻ trộm"; ordination strips caste and leaves only "sa-môn".
- The highly repetitive query/answer blocks mirror the root segment-for-segment, including per-segment ellipsis "…" where the root elides repeated caste enumerations.
- Ends with the earliest-style refuge after the Buddha's parinibbāna: Mahākaccāna redirects the king's refuge to the quenched Buddha.
- "dhammikaṁ rakkhāvaraṇaguttiṁ" = việc bảo vệ che chở chính đáng.
