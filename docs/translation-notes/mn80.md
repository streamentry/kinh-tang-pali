## Notes

- From-scratch translation (101 segments). Twin of MN 79: the wanderer Vekhanasa repeats the "ultimate splendor" creed, is refuted via the same finest-lady simile and the ascending radiance series (beryl → firefly → lamp → bonfire → morning star → full moon → sun, capped by higher gods), then receives the five sensual stimuli and the three-tier sensual-pleasure teaching (senses → sensual pleasure → pleasure surmounting the sensual, "dục tối thượng lạc" per TMC).
- Vocatives per root: the Buddha addresses Vekhanasa as "kaccāna" (này Kaccāna); Vekhanasa addresses "bho gotama" (thưa Tôn giả Gotama, per TMC).
- New canonical elements beyond MN 79: five sensual stimuli with the MN 26:31.3 adjective set; the arahant-epithet list per MN 1:51.1; the baby bound at the neck simile (release from the bond of ignorance); the lay-follower declaration; and the closing chapter colophon + uddāna verses (18.0–18.4, P present / EN empty — translated as name mnemonics with documented uncertainty in the comment file).
- "samaṇo gotamo pāpito bhavissatī" rendered "Sa-môn Gotama sẽ bị thua thiệt" (Sujato: will be worsted; TMC reads it as "sẽ bị đọa lạc" — difference documented in comment).
