## Notes

- From-scratch translation (150 segments). A grieving father rejects "sầu, bi, khổ, ưu, não sinh từ những điều thân yêu" (gamblers agree with him), Queen Mallikā verifies with the Buddha, then proves it to the king via his own attachments (daughter, wife, son, herself, and the Kāsi-Kosala realms); the king's triple homage closes the sutta.
- The central formula renders TMC's reading adjusted to the sutta's examples: "sinh từ những điều thân yêu, hiện hữu từ nơi những điều thân yêu" (piyajātikā … piyappabhavikā).
- The mad-grief similes (streets and squares) and the gruesome husband/wife episode follow the root's combined segment blocks (9-14.x, 15-21.x) with per-segment ellipsis mirrors.
