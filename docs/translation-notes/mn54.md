## High-risk audit points

- `vohārasamuccheda` is kept visibly consistent as “dứt bỏ các việc thế tục” so the dialogue’s deliberate redefinition is not lost.
- The eight practices remain distinct and each causal explanation retains fetter, hindrance, self-reproach, criticism by the wise, rebirth consequence, and the distressing/feverish influxes.
- The seven similes for sensual pleasures are complete: skeleton, scrap of meat, grass torch, charcoal pit, dream, borrowed goods, and fruit tree.
- `upekkhā` based on diversity versus unity is not over-explained through a later commentarial system.
- Past-life recollection, divine eye, kamma/rebirth, and liberation claims are translated as textual claims rather than naturalized.
- The `ājānīya` thoroughbred metaphor is preserved rather than replaced with a modern analogy.
