## Notes

- From-scratch translation (152 segments). The Buddha teaches noble right samādhi "cùng với các nhân và các trợ cụ" — unification of mind equipped with the other seven factors, with right view "đi trước" in each pair. For right view, thought, speech, action and livelihood, each is twofold: the mundane ("có lậu hoặc, thuộc về phước, cảm quả nơi thủ") and the transcendent ("Thánh, vô lậu, xuất thế gian, là một chi của đạo"), with parallel analyses (wisdom faculty/power/investigation-awakening-factor; applied placing of mind; desisting-abstaining-refraining). Each factor is orbited by the triad right view-effort-mindfulness. The ten-factor sequence (right view → right knowledge → right freedom) gives the trainee eight factors and the arahant ten. The "Great Forty" — twenty skillful and twenty unskillful sides, each right factor wearing away its wrong counterpart and its dependent unskillful states — has been rolled forth irreversibly; any would-be critic earns ten legitimate rebuttals (praising those of wrong view etc.), and even the Okkāla annihilationists feared blame too much to reject it.
