## Notes

- From-scratch translation (159 segments). King Pasenadi, reminded of the Buddha by quiet tree roots, travels three leagues to Medaḷumpa, hands his sword and turban to Dīgha Kārāyana, and bows to the Buddha's feet, kissing them and announcing his name. Asked why, he offers six "shrines to the teaching" (dhammacetiya — rendered "tháp pháp"): lifelong purity of the mendicants, the Sangha's harmony "như sữa với nước", their deer-like contentment, the rodless training of assemblies (the cough anecdote), the conversion of disputants, and the chamberlains Isidatta & Purāṇa sleeping head-toward-the-Buddha. He closes by noting shared caste, homeland, and age (both 80).
- "dhammanvayo" = "con suy xét về Đức Phật theo giáo pháp như sau" (TMC reads "pháp truyền thống" — documented); each shrine ends with the triple-gem formula.
- Buddha's instruction: learn, memorize, remember these "tháp pháp" — beneficial and fundamental to the holy life.
