## Notes

- From-scratch translation (117 segments). On the Uposatha full-moon night, seated in the open amid the Saṅgha, the Buddha takes a monk's catechism on the five grasping aggregates: their list ("sắc thủ uẩn… thức thủ uẩn" per TMC), their root ("lấy dục làm gốc"), grasping as neither identical with nor apart from them (desire-and-greed for them is the grasping), disparity of that desire through future-becoming wishes, the twenty-aspect scope of each "uẩn", their causes (four elements for form, contact for feeling-perception-choices, name-and-form for consciousness), identity view arising and not arising through the twenty affirmations/negations, and gratification-drawback-escape for each aggregate. Then the "knowing and seeing" formula dissolving ego-making, mine-making and conceit-inclination "trong thân có thức này và ở ngoài, đối với mọi tướng" via the not-mine contemplation. A monk's wrong thought ("nghiệp do vô ngã tạo ra, ai sẽ thọ quả?") is mind-read and answered by the trained interrogation: impermanent → suffering → not worth regarding as "của tôi, là tôi, tự ngã của tôi" (corpus wordings from mn22/35/70 reused), ending in disillusion → dispassion → liberation, with sixty monks' minds freed by not grasping.
