## Notes

- From-scratch translation (143 segments). The brahmins' four prescribed services and four prescribed wealths (per caste, each with the "guard who steals" censure) are both rejected — nobody authorized them; the forced-meat simile. The Buddha's counter: serve whoever makes you grow in faith/ethics/learning/generosity/wisdom; noble transcendent wealth (12.1); caste names are mere labels by birth-place, like fire named for its fuel (12.7–12.11); going forth from any caste succeeds equally in the skillful teaching, corroborated by the mettā, bathing, and fire-drill arguments (shared with MN 93).
