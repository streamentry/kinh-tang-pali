## Notes

- From-scratch translation (128 segments). The Buddha first establishes his teaching motive with the monks (not for robes, almsfood, lodging, or "sự tái sinh kiểu này kiểu kia", but "Thế Tôn là bậc từ mẫn, mong muốn lợi ích; thuyết pháp xuất phát từ lòng từ mẫn"), then lays down community protocols: train together in the 37 aids (list reuses DN28:3.3 wording) "trong hòa hợp, quý mến nhau, không tranh luận"; a four-case protocol for two monks disagreeing on meaning/phrasing (approach the most amenable monk of each party, name the disagreement, forbid dispute — "Còn văn chỉ là việc nhỏ nhặt" — and remember right/wrong memorization as such before speaking Dhamma and Vinaya); an offense protocol (don't rush to accuse; examine the person across trouble-to-self / harm-to-other × temperament / reformability, with "chớ xem thường sự xả" when nothing can be done); and a quarrel liturgy asking each side whether "vị sa-môn" would criticize the resurfaced quarrels and whether without giving them up "liệu có thể chứng ngộ Niết-bàn" — answered in the negative. Closes with the peacemaker's account (approached the Buddha, relayed the teaching, monks were established in the skillful) and the standard of answering without self-glorification.
- Place: root "pisinārāyaṁ baliharaṇe vanasaṇḍe" rendered "ở Kusinārā, trong khu rừng Baliharaṇa" following the commentary note (as TMC and Sujato do).
