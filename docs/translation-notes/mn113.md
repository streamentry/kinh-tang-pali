## Notes

- From-scratch translation (218 segments, including the range keys `mn113:4-6.*` and `mn113:16-20.*` and `mn113:22-24.*`). The true-person/untrue-person catalogue in twenty pairs: twelve worldly qualities (eminent/great/wealthy/exceptionally-wealthy family, fame, material gains, learning, Vinaya expertise, Dhamma-teaching, forest-dwelling, rag-robe, alms-eating, tree-root, charnel-ground/open-air/sitters/any-seat/one-session) and eight attainments (four jhānas, three formless dimensions). In each pair the untrue person boasts "Tôi là…, còn những tỳ-kheo khác này không…" while the true person sees that greed-hate-delusion don't end through such things, honors only practice-in-line-with-Dhamma, and — for the attainments — recalls the Buddha's word of `atammayatā` ("pháp vô tham ái"; "với cái nào họ quán niệm, thì với cái ấy trở thành khác đi"), keeping non-enthrallment as the measure. The series culminates in the true person entering cessation of perception and feeling with defilements ended — "không quán niệm gì, không quán niệm ở đâu, không quán niệm vì cái gì".
