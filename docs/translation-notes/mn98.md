## Notes

- From-scratch translation (294 segments). The students Vāseṭṭha (brahmin by conduct) and Bhāradvāja (brahmin by seven-generation lineage) bring their dispute to the Buddha. Verse answer: species of grass, insects, quadrupeds, snakes, fish, and birds differ by birth-markers, but humans do not — differences are conventional naming; the eight occupations (farmer, craftsman, trader, servant, thief, warrior, sacrificer, king) define a person, not brahminhood; then the ~40-fold "Ta gọi người ấy là Bà-la-môn" refrain (parallel to Snp 3.4); the conclusion: "nhờ nghiệp là Bà-la-môn", deeds drive the world like a chariot's linchpin; the supreme brahmin = three knowledges, rebirth ended — "đối kẻ hiểu biết, người ấy là Phạm thiên và Sakka". Both take refuge.
- Verse lines follow the root's two-pāda segmentation 1:1; TMC consulted for the refrain idioms (lá sen / hạt cải / chốt xe / bò mộng).
