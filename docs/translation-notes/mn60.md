## High-risk audit points

- The `apaṇṇaka` reasoning is not flattened into a modern “Pascal's wager”. The discourse also explicitly asserts the other world, efficacy of action, and causality in its evaluation of the opposed views.
- The first wrong-view formula preserves all items in Pāli, including this world/other world, mother/father, spontaneous rebirth, and direct knowers of the other world.
- `akiriyavāda` is kept as denial of moral efficacy of action, not a claim that physical actions literally do not occur.
- The ahetuka/determinist view preserves both denial of causes for purification/corruption and denial of human power/effort.
- The sections on formless attainments and cessation of becoming preserve epistemic restraint: the sensible person does not claim direct knowledge they lack.
- The long `…pe…` stock passages are expanded from known Majjhima parallels rather than hallucinated, while ancient lexical uncertainties are handled conservatively.
- Rebirth, heaven, hell, formless devas, and parinibbāna are translated as textual claims, not silently allegorized.
