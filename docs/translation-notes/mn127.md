## Notes

- From-scratch translation (147 segments, zero exact corpus matches). Structure: the chamberlain Pañcakaṅga invites Anuruddha to a meal ("bữa ăn thứ tư") and asks: senior monks tell him to develop the limitless release of heart, others the expansive release — same meaning or different? Anuruddha makes him state his view (same), then corrects: they differ in meaning and expression. Limitless = the four brahmavihāras suffusing the whole world (mn43 wording reused); expansive = pervading "đại hành" through a ladder of scopes — one tree root, two-three, one village district, two-three, one kingdom, two-three, the ocean-bounded earth. Then four rebirths: pervading "limited/limitless/corrupted/pure luminosity" leads to corresponding radiant gods. The gods gathered show color-difference but not radiance-difference (many oil lamps in one house); dispersed, both (lamps carried out); they don't think "thường còn, thường trú, thường hằng" — like flies carried in a basket. Sabhiya Kaccāna asks why one order of gods differs; Anuruddha walks him up the six-rung expansive ladder in counter-question; then the impure/pure oil-lamp simile (bodily discomfort, dullness-drowsiness, restlessness-remorse → dim absorption → corrupted radiance). Kaccāna infers Anuruddha speaks from having lived with those gods; Anuruddha calls the remark intrusive but confirms it.
