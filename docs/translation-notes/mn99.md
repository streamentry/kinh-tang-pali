## Notes

- From-scratch translation (270 segments). Subha Todeyya's son raises three brahmin claims: lay-vs-renunciate success (answered with "Ta là bậc nói sau khi phân tích" and the right/wrong practice criterion); the greatness of household work (four-case farming/trade analysis); and the five merit-making things (truth, fervor, chastity, recitation, generosity). The blind-queue simile provokes Subha's Pokkharasāti citation, dismantled by Subha's own admission (can't read his maid Puṇṇikā's mind), the blind-from-birth simile, the four speech criteria, the hindrances and sense-pleasures analysis; the fire simile for rapture apart from sensual pleasures; the "sixth ground" of sympathy; the five things as "phương tiện trợ ích cho tâm"; the Naḷakāra road simile and the four divine abidings with the conch simile ("nghiệp có hạn lượng không tồn tại"). Coda: Jāṇussoṇi's praise and salutation.
- Shared formulas reuse the published MN 95/97 wordings (blind queue, ancient seers list, four abidings).
