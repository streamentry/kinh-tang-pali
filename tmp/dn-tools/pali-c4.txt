--- dn24:2.14.0
P: 6. Aggaññapaññattikathā 

--- dn24:2.14.1
P: Aggaññañcāhaṁ, bhaggava, pajānāmi. 

--- dn24:2.14.2
P: Tañca pajānāmi, tato ca uttaritaraṁ pajānāmi, tañca pajānaṁ na parāmasāmi, aparāmasato ca me paccattaññeva nibbuti viditā, yadabhijānaṁ tathāgato no anayaṁ āpajjati. 

--- dn24:2.14.3
P: Santi, bhaggava, eke samaṇabrāhmaṇā issarakuttaṁ brahmakuttaṁ ācariyakaṁ aggaññaṁ paññapenti. 

--- dn24:2.14.4

--- dn24:2.14.5

--- dn24:2.14.6

--- dn24:2.14.7
P: Tyāhaṁ evaṁ vadāmi: 

--- dn24:2.14.8
P: ‘kathaṁvihitakaṁ pana tumhe āyasmanto issarakuttaṁ brahmakuttaṁ ācariyakaṁ aggaññaṁ paññapethā’ti? 

--- dn24:2.14.9
P: Te mayā puṭṭhā na sampāyanti, asampāyantā mamaññeva paṭipucchanti.
