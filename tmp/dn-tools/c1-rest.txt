dn16:1.24.0
P: 6. Sīlavantaānisaṁsa
EN: 6. The Benefits of Ethical Conduct

dn16:1.24.1
P: Pañcime, gahapatayo, ānisaṁsā sīlavato sīlasampadāya.
EN: There are these five benefits for an ethical person because of their accomplishment in ethics.

dn16:1.24.2
P: Katame pañca?
EN: What five?

dn16:1.24.3
P: Idha, gahapatayo, sīlavā sīlasampanno appamādādhikaraṇaṁ mahantaṁ bhogakkhandhaṁ adhigacchati.
EN: Firstly, an ethical person gains great wealth on account of diligence.

dn16:1.24.4
P: Ayaṁ paṭhamo ānisaṁso sīlavato sīlasampadāya.
EN: This is the first benefit.

dn16:1.24.5
P: Puna caparaṁ, gahapatayo, sīlavato sīlasampannassa kalyāṇo kittisaddo abbhuggacchati.
EN: Furthermore, an ethical person gets a good reputation.

dn16:1.24.6
P: Ayaṁ dutiyo ānisaṁso sīlavato sīlasampadāya.
EN: This is the second benefit.

dn16:1.24.7
P: Puna caparaṁ, gahapatayo, sīlavā sīlasampanno yaññadeva parisaṁ upasaṅkamati—yadi khattiyaparisaṁ yadi brāhmaṇaparisaṁ yadi gahapatiparisaṁ yadi samaṇaparisaṁ visārado upasaṅkamati amaṅkubhūto.
EN: Furthermore, an ethical person enters any kind of assembly bold and self-assured, whether it’s an assembly of aristocrats, brahmins, householders, or ascetics.

dn16:1.24.8
P: Ayaṁ tatiyo ānisaṁso sīlavato sīlasampadāya.
EN: This is the third benefit.

dn16:1.24.9
P: Puna caparaṁ, gahapatayo, sīlavā sīlasampanno asammūḷho kālaṁ karoti.
EN: Furthermore, an ethical person dies not feeling lost.

dn16:1.24.10
P: Ayaṁ catuttho ānisaṁso sīlavato sīlasampadāya.
EN: This is the fourth benefit.

dn16:1.24.11
P: Puna caparaṁ, gahapatayo, sīlavā sīlasampanno kāyassa bhedā paraṁ maraṇā sugatiṁ saggaṁ lokaṁ upapajjati.
EN: Furthermore, when an ethical person’s body breaks up, after death, they’re reborn in a good place, a heavenly realm.

dn16:1.24.12
P: Ayaṁ pañcamo ānisaṁso sīlavato sīlasampadāya.
EN: This is the fifth benefit.

dn16:1.24.13
P: Ime kho, gahapatayo, pañca ānisaṁsā sīlavato sīlasampadāyā”ti.
EN: These are the five benefits for an ethical person because of their accomplishment in ethics.”
