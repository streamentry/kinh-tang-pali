--- dn24:1.19.0
P: 5. Iddhipāṭihāriyakathā 

--- dn24:1.19.1
P: Atha khvāhaṁ, bhaggava, pubbaṇhasamayaṁ nivāsetvā pattacīvaramādāya vesāliṁ piṇḍāya pāvisiṁ. 

--- dn24:1.19.2
P: Vesāliyaṁ piṇḍāya caritvā pacchābhattaṁ piṇḍapātapaṭikkanto yena acelassa pāthikaputtassa ārāmo tenupasaṅkamiṁ divāvihārāya. 

--- dn24:1.19.3
P: Atha kho, bhaggava, sunakkhatto licchaviputto taramānarūpo vesāliṁ pavisitvā yena abhiññātā abhiññātā licchavī tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā abhiññāte abhiññāte licchavī etadavoca: 

--- dn24:1.19.4
P: ‘esāvuso, bhagavā vesāliyaṁ piṇḍāya caritvā pacchābhattaṁ piṇḍapātapaṭikkanto yena acelassa pāthikaputtassa ārāmo tenupasaṅkami divāvihārāya. 

--- dn24:1.19.5
P: Abhikkamathāyasmanto abhikkamathāyasmanto, sādhurūpānaṁ samaṇānaṁ uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ bhavissatī’ti. 

--- dn24:1.19.6
P: Atha kho, bhaggava, abhiññātānaṁ abhiññātānaṁ licchavīnaṁ etadahosi: 

--- dn24:1.19.7
P: ‘sādhurūpānaṁ kira, bho, samaṇānaṁ uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ bhavissati; 

--- dn24:1.19.8
P: handa vata, bho, gacchāmā’ti. 

--- dn24:1.19.9
P: Yena ca abhiññātā abhiññātā brāhmaṇamahāsālā gahapatinecayikā nānātitthiyā samaṇabrāhmaṇā tenupasaṅkami. upasaṅkamitvā abhiññāte abhiññāte nānātitthiye samaṇabrāhmaṇe etadavoca: 

--- dn24:1.19.10
P: ‘esāvuso, bhagavā vesāliyaṁ piṇḍāya caritvā pacchābhattaṁ piṇḍapātapaṭikkanto yena acelassa pāthikaputtassa ārāmo tenupasaṅkami divāvihārāya. 

--- dn24:1.19.11
P: Abhikkamathāyasmanto abhikkamathāyasmanto, sādhurūpānaṁ samaṇānaṁ uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ bhavissatī’ti. 

--- dn24:1.19.12
P: Atha kho, bhaggava, abhiññātānaṁ abhiññātānaṁ nānātitthiyānaṁ samaṇabrāhmaṇānaṁ etadahosi: 

--- dn24:1.19.13
P: ‘sādhurūpānaṁ kira, bho, samaṇānaṁ uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ bhavissati; 

--- dn24:1.19.14
P: handa vata, bho, gacchāmā’ti.

--- dn24:1.20.16

--- dn24:1.20.17

--- dn24:1.20.18
P: Cattāri ce samaṇo gotamo uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyāni karissati, aṭṭhāhaṁ karissāmi. 

--- dn24:1.20.19

P: Evampi kho, bhaggava, vuccamāno acelo pāthikaputto ‘āyāmi, āvuso, āyāmi, āvuso’ti vatvā tattheva saṁsappati, na sakkoti āsanāpi vuṭṭhātunti.
