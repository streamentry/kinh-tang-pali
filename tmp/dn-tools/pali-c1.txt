P: Dīgha Nikāya 24 

--- dn24:0.2
P: Pāthikasutta 

--- dn24:1.0
P: 1. Sunakkhattavatthu 

--- dn24:1.1.1
P: Evaṁ me sutaṁ—

--- dn24:1.1.2
P: ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā mallesu viharati anupiyaṁ nāma mallānaṁ nigamo. 

--- dn24:1.1.3
P: Atha kho bhagavā pubbaṇhasamayaṁ nivāsetvā pattacīvaramādāya anupiyaṁ piṇḍāya pāvisi. 

--- dn24:1.1.4
P: Atha kho bhagavato etadahosi: 

--- dn24:1.1.5
P: “atippago kho tāva anupiyāyaṁ piṇḍāya carituṁ. 

--- dn24:1.1.6
P: Yannūnāhaṁ yena bhaggavagottassa paribbājakassa ārāmo, yena bhaggavagotto paribbājako tenupasaṅkameyyan”ti. 

--- dn24:1.2.1
P: Atha kho bhagavā yena bhaggavagottassa paribbājakassa ārāmo, yena bhaggavagotto paribbājako tenupasaṅkami. 

--- dn24:1.2.2
P: Atha kho bhaggavagotto paribbājako bhagavantaṁ etadavoca: 

--- dn24:1.2.3
P: “etu kho, bhante, bhagavā. 

--- dn24:1.2.4
P: Svāgataṁ, bhante, bhagavato. 

--- dn24:1.2.5
P: Cirassaṁ kho, bhante, bhagavā imaṁ pariyāyamakāsi yadidaṁ idhāgamanāya. 

--- dn24:1.2.6
P: Nisīdatu, bhante, bhagavā, idamāsanaṁ paññattan”ti. 

--- dn24:1.2.7
P: Nisīdi bhagavā paññatte āsane. 

--- dn24:1.2.8
P: Bhaggavagottopi kho paribbājako aññataraṁ nīcaṁ āsanaṁ gahetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. 

--- dn24:1.2.9
P: Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho bhaggavagotto paribbājako bhagavantaṁ etadavoca: 

--- dn24:1.2.10
P: “purimāni, bhante, divasāni purimatarāni sunakkhatto licchaviputto yenāhaṁ tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā maṁ etadavoca: 

--- dn24:1.2.11
P: ‘paccakkhāto dāni mayā, bhaggava, bhagavā. 

--- dn24:1.2.12
P: Na dānāhaṁ bhagavantaṁ uddissa viharāmī’ti. 

--- dn24:1.2.13
P: Kaccetaṁ, bhante, tatheva, yathā sunakkhatto licchaviputto avacā”ti? 

--- dn24:1.2.14
P: “Tatheva kho etaṁ, bhaggava, yathā sunakkhatto licchaviputto avaca. 

--- dn24:1.3.1
P: Purimāni, bhaggava, divasāni purimatarāni sunakkhatto licchaviputto yenāhaṁ tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā maṁ abhivādetvā ekamantaṁ nisīdi. Ekamantaṁ nisinno kho, bhaggava, sunakkhatto licchaviputto maṁ etadavoca: 

--- dn24:1.3.2
P: ‘paccakkhāmi dānāhaṁ, bhante, bhagavantaṁ. 

--- dn24:1.3.3
P: Na dānāhaṁ, bhante, bhagavantaṁ uddissa viharissāmī’ti. 

--- dn24:1.3.4
P: Evaṁ vutte, ahaṁ, bhaggava, sunakkhattaṁ licchaviputtaṁ etadavocaṁ: 

--- dn24:1.3.5
P: ‘api nu tāhaṁ, sunakkhatta, evaṁ avacaṁ, 

--- dn24:1.3.6
P: ehi tvaṁ, sunakkhatta, mamaṁ uddissa viharāhī’ti? 

--- dn24:1.3.7
P: ‘No hetaṁ, bhante’. 

--- dn24:1.3.8
P: ‘Tvaṁ vā pana maṁ evaṁ avaca—

--- dn24:1.3.9
P: ahaṁ, bhante, bhagavantaṁ uddissa viharissāmī’ti? 

--- dn24:1.3.10
P: ‘No hetaṁ, bhante’. 

--- dn24:1.3.11
P: ‘Iti kira, sunakkhatta, nevāhaṁ taṁ vadāmi—

--- dn24:1.3.12
P: ehi tvaṁ, sunakkhatta, mamaṁ uddissa viharāhīti. 

--- dn24:1.3.13
P: Napi kira maṁ tvaṁ vadesi—

--- dn24:1.3.14
P: ahaṁ, bhante, bhagavantaṁ uddissa viharissāmīti. 

--- dn24:1.3.15
P: Evaṁ sante, moghapurisa, ko santo kaṁ paccācikkhasi? 

--- dn24:1.3.16
P: Passa, moghapurisa, yāvañca te idaṁ aparaddhan’ti. 

--- dn24:1.4.1
P: ‘Na hi pana me, bhante, bhagavā uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ karotī’ti. 

--- dn24:1.4.2
P: ‘Api nu tāhaṁ, sunakkhatta, evaṁ avacaṁ—

--- dn24:1.4.3
P: ehi tvaṁ, sunakkhatta, mamaṁ uddissa viharāhi, ahaṁ te uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ karissāmī’ti? 

--- dn24:1.4.4
P: ‘No hetaṁ, bhante’. 

--- dn24:1.4.5
P: ‘Tvaṁ vā pana maṁ evaṁ avaca—

--- dn24:1.4.6
P: ahaṁ, bhante, bhagavantaṁ uddissa viharissāmi, bhagavā me uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ karissatī’ti? 

--- dn24:1.4.7
P: ‘No hetaṁ, bhante’. 

--- dn24:1.4.8
P: ‘Iti kira, sunakkhatta, nevāhaṁ taṁ vadāmi—

--- dn24:1.4.9
P: ehi tvaṁ, sunakkhatta, mamaṁ uddissa viharāhi, ahaṁ te uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ karissāmīti; 

--- dn24:1.4.10
P: napi kira maṁ tvaṁ vadesi—

--- dn24:1.4.11
P: ahaṁ, bhante, bhagavantaṁ uddissa viharissāmi, bhagavā me uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ karissatīti. 

--- dn24:1.4.12
P: Evaṁ sante, moghapurisa, ko santo kaṁ paccācikkhasi? 

--- dn24:1.4.13
P: Taṁ kiṁ maññasi, sunakkhatta, 

--- dn24:1.4.14
P: kate vā uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriye akate vā uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriye yassatthāya mayā dhammo desito so niyyāti takkarassa sammā dukkhakkhayāyā’ti? 

--- dn24:1.4.15
P: ‘Kate vā, bhante, uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriye akate vā uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriye yassatthāya bhagavatā dhammo desito so niyyāti takkarassa sammā dukkhakkhayāyā’ti. 

--- dn24:1.4.16
P: ‘Iti kira, sunakkhatta, kate vā uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriye, akate vā uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriye, yassatthāya mayā dhammo desito, so niyyāti takkarassa sammā dukkhakkhayāya. 

--- dn24:1.4.17
P: Tatra, sunakkhatta, kiṁ uttari manussadhammā iddhipāṭihāriyaṁ kataṁ karissati? 

--- dn24:1.4.18
P: Passa, moghapurisa, yāvañca te idaṁ aparaddhan’ti. 

--- dn24:1.5.1
P: ‘Na hi pana me, bhante, bhagavā aggaññaṁ paññapetī’ti? 

--- dn24:1.5.2
P: ‘Api nu tāhaṁ, sunakkhatta, evaṁ avacaṁ—

--- dn24:1.5.3
P: ehi tvaṁ, sunakkhatta, mamaṁ uddissa viharāhi, ahaṁ te aggaññaṁ paññapessāmī’ti? 

--- dn24:1.5.4
P: ‘No hetaṁ, bhante’. 

--- dn24:1.5.5
P: ‘Tvaṁ vā pana maṁ evaṁ avaca—

--- dn24:1.5.6
P: ahaṁ, bhante, bhagavantaṁ uddissa viharissāmi, bhagavā me aggaññaṁ paññapessatī’ti? 

--- dn24:1.5.7
P: ‘No hetaṁ, bhante’. 

--- dn24:1.5.8
P: ‘Iti kira, sunakkhatta, nevāhaṁ taṁ vadāmi—

--- dn24:1.5.9
P: ehi tvaṁ, sunakkhatta, mamaṁ uddissa viharāhi, ahaṁ te aggaññaṁ paññapessāmīti. 

--- dn24:1.5.10
P: Napi kira maṁ tvaṁ vadesi—

--- dn24:1.5.11
P: ahaṁ, bhante, bhagavantaṁ uddissa viharissāmi, bhagavā me aggaññaṁ paññapessatīti. 

--- dn24:1.5.12
P: Evaṁ sante, moghapurisa, ko santo kaṁ paccācikkhasi? 

--- dn24:1.5.13
P: Taṁ kiṁ maññasi, sunakkhatta, 

--- dn24:1.5.14
P: paññatte vā aggaññe, apaññatte vā aggaññe, yassatthāya mayā dhammo desito, so niyyāti takkarassa sammā dukkhakkhayāyā’ti? 

--- dn24:1.5.15
P: ‘Paññatte vā, bhante, aggaññe, apaññatte vā aggaññe, yassatthāya bhagavatā dhammo desito, so niyyāti takkarassa sammā dukkhakkhayāyā’ti. 

--- dn24:1.5.16
P: ‘Iti kira, sunakkhatta, paññatte vā aggaññe, apaññatte vā aggaññe, yassatthāya mayā dhammo desito, so niyyāti takkarassa sammā dukkhakkhayāya. 

--- dn24:1.5.17
P: Tatra, sunakkhatta, kiṁ aggaññaṁ paññattaṁ karissati? 

--- dn24:1.5.18
P: Passa, moghapurisa, yāvañca te idaṁ aparaddhaṁ. 

--- dn24:1.6.1
P: Anekapariyāyena kho te, sunakkhatta, mama vaṇṇo bhāsito vajjigāme—

--- dn24:1.6.2
P: itipi so bhagavā arahaṁ sammāsambuddho vijjācaraṇasampanno sugato lokavidū anuttaro purisadammasārathi satthā devamanussānaṁ buddho bhagavāti. 

--- dn24:1.6.3
P: Iti kho te, sunakkhatta, anekapariyāyena mama vaṇṇo bhāsito vajjigāme. 

--- dn24:1.6.4
P: Anekapariyāyena kho te, sunakkhatta, dhammassa vaṇṇo bhāsito vajjigāme—

--- dn24:1.6.5
P: svākkhāto bhagavatā dhammo sandiṭṭhiko akāliko ehipassiko opaneyyiko paccattaṁ veditabbo viññūhīti. 

--- dn24:1.6.6
P: Iti kho te, sunakkhatta, anekapariyāyena dhammassa vaṇṇo bhāsito vajjigāme. 

--- dn24:1.6.7
P: Anekapariyāyena kho te, sunakkhatta, saṅghassa vaṇṇo bhāsito vajjigāme—

--- dn24:1.6.8
P: suppaṭipanno bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho, ujuppaṭipanno bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho, ñāyappaṭipanno bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho, sāmīcippaṭipanno bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho, yadidaṁ cattāri purisayugāni aṭṭha purisapuggalā, esa bhagavato sāvakasaṅgho, āhuneyyo pāhuneyyo dakkhiṇeyyo añjalikaraṇīyo anuttaraṁ puññakkhettaṁ lokassāti. 

--- dn24:1.6.9
P: Iti kho te, sunakkhatta, anekapariyāyena saṅghassa vaṇṇo bhāsito vajjigāme. 

--- dn24:1.6.10
P: Ārocayāmi kho te, sunakkhatta, paṭivedayāmi kho te, sunakkhatta. 

--- dn24:1.6.11
P: Bhavissanti kho te, sunakkhatta, vattāro, no visahi sunakkhatto licchaviputto samaṇe gotame brahmacariyaṁ carituṁ, so avisahanto sikkhaṁ paccakkhāya hīnāyāvattoti. 

--- dn24:1.6.12
P: Iti kho te, sunakkhatta, bhavissanti vattāro’ti. 

--- dn24:1.6.13
P: Evaṁ kho, bhaggava, sunakkhatto licchaviputto mayā vuccamāno apakkameva imasmā dhammavinayā, yathā taṁ āpāyiko nerayiko. 

--- dn24:1.7.0
P: 2. Korakkhattiyavatthu 

--- dn24:1.7.1
P: Ekamidāhaṁ, bhaggava, samayaṁ thūlūsu viharāmi uttarakā nāma thūlūnaṁ nigamo. 

--- dn24:1.7.2
P: Atha khvāhaṁ, bhaggava, pubbaṇhasamayaṁ nivāsetvā pattacīvaramādāya sunakkhattena licchaviputtena pacchāsamaṇena uttarakaṁ piṇḍāya pāvisiṁ. 

--- dn24:1.7.3
P: Tena kho pana samayena acelo korakkhattiyo kukkuravatiko catukkuṇḍiko chamānikiṇṇaṁ bhakkhasaṁ mukheneva khādati, mukheneva bhuñjati. 

--- dn24:1.7.4
P: Addasā kho, bhaggava, sunakkhatto licchaviputto acelaṁ korakkhattiyaṁ kukkuravatikaṁ catukkuṇḍikaṁ chamānikiṇṇaṁ bhakkhasaṁ mukheneva khādantaṁ mukheneva bhuñjantaṁ. 

--- dn24:1.7.5
P: Disvānassa etadahosi: 

--- dn24:1.7.6
P: ‘sādhurūpo vata bho ayaṁ samaṇo catukkuṇḍiko chamānikiṇṇaṁ bhakkhasaṁ mukheneva khādati, mukheneva bhuñjatī’ti. 

--- dn24:1.7.7
P: Atha khvāhaṁ, bhaggava, sunakkhattassa licchaviputtassa cetasā cetoparivitakkamaññāya sunakkhattaṁ licchaviputtaṁ etadavocaṁ: 

--- dn24:1.7.8
P: ‘tvampi nāma, moghapurisa, samaṇo sakyaputtiyo paṭijānissasī’ti. 

--- dn24:1.7.9
P: ‘Kiṁ pana maṁ, bhante, bhagavā evamāha: 

--- dn24:1.7.10
P: “tvampi nāma, moghapurisa, samaṇo sakyaputtiyo paṭijānissasī”’ti? 

--- dn24:1.7.11
P: ‘Nanu te, sunakkhatta, imaṁ acelaṁ korakkhattiyaṁ kukkuravatikaṁ catukkuṇḍikaṁ chamānikiṇṇaṁ bhakkhasaṁ mukheneva khādantaṁ mukheneva bhuñjantaṁ disvāna etadahosi—

--- dn24:1.7.12
P: sādhurūpo vata bho ayaṁ samaṇo catukkuṇḍiko chamānikiṇṇaṁ bhakkhasaṁ mukheneva khādati, mukheneva bhuñjatī’ti? 

--- dn24:1.7.13
P: ‘Evaṁ, bhante. 

--- dn24:1.7.14
P: Kiṁ pana, bhante, bhagavā arahattassa maccharāyatī’ti? 

--- dn24:1.7.15
P: ‘Na kho ahaṁ, moghapurisa, arahattassa maccharāyāmi. 

--- dn24:1.7.16
P: Api ca tuyhevetaṁ pāpakaṁ diṭṭhigataṁ uppannaṁ, taṁ pajaha. 

--- dn24:1.7.17
P: Mā te ahosi dīgharattaṁ ahitāya dukkhāya. 

--- dn24:1.7.18
P: Yaṁ kho panetaṁ, sunakkhatta, maññasi acelaṁ korakkhattiyaṁ—sādhurūpo ayaṁ samaṇoti. So sattamaṁ divasaṁ alasakena kālaṁ karissati. 

--- dn24:1.7.19
P: Kālaṅkato ca kālakañcikā nāma asurā sabbanihīno asurakāyo, tatra upapajjissati. 

--- dn24:1.7.20
P: Kālaṅkatañca naṁ bīraṇatthambake susāne chaḍḍessanti. 

--- dn24:1.7.21
P: Ākaṅkhamāno ca tvaṁ, sunakkhatta, acelaṁ korakkhattiyaṁ upasaṅkamitvā puccheyyāsi—

--- dn24:1.7.22
P: jānāsi, āvuso korakkhattiya, attano gatinti? 

--- dn24:1.7.23
P: Ṭhānaṁ kho panetaṁ, sunakkhatta, vijjati yaṁ te acelo korakkhattiyo byākarissati—

--- dn24:1.7.24
P: jānāmi, āvuso sunakkhatta, attano gatiṁ; 

--- dn24:1.7.25
P: kālakañcikā nāma asurā sabbanihīno asurakāyo, tatrāmhi upapanno’ti.
